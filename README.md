# YojnaMitra 🇮🇳

### AI-Powered Government Scheme Assistant

YojnaMitra is a web-based platform designed to help citizens discover, understand, and access government welfare schemes offered by the Central and State Governments of India.

The platform aims to simplify scheme discovery by helping users explore relevant schemes based on their personal profiles, understand eligibility requirements, and access official application resources.

## 📌 Project Overview

Many citizens find it difficult to identify government welfare schemes because information is spread across different government websites, portals, and documents.

YojnaMitra aims to bring this information into one accessible platform. Users can create a profile, explore government schemes, and access information about eligibility, benefits, required documents, and application procedures.

The project also plans to use AI-powered assistance and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to make scheme information easier to understand.

## ✨ Features

* **User Authentication:** Sign up and log in to access the platform.
* **User Profile:** Store basic details such as age, state, district, education, occupation, and family income.
* **Government Scheme Discovery:** Explore government welfare schemes through a centralized interface.
* **Scheme Information:** View scheme descriptions, benefits, eligibility requirements, and application guidance.
* **Eligibility Assistance:** Support users in understanding scheme eligibility.
* **Bilingual Interface:** Switch between English and Hindi.
* **Account Management:** View account details and sign out.
* **Responsive UI:** Access the platform on desktop and mobile devices.

> **Development status:** Profile-based scheme matching, verified government scheme data integration, and AI-powered RAG assistance are planned implementation areas. Features that have not yet been implemented should not be considered operational.
